# FOCUS Scheme — Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. What is FOCUS?
FOCUS stands for "Farmers' Collectivization for Upscaling Production and Marketing Systems." It is a flagship farmer-welfare scheme of the Government of Meghalaya that organises farmers into **Producer Groups (PGs)** and provides them financial assistance and value-chain support to upscale their farming and income-generating activities.

### 2. When was FOCUS launched?
It was launched during 2021, in response to the economic hardship faced by farmers during the COVID-19 pandemic. Different official sources cite slightly different months — some say March 2021, while a Chief Minister's statement in February 2022 referred to it as having been launched "in September last year" (i.e., September 2021). The exact date should be confirmed with an official notification if precision matters.

### 3. Who runs the FOCUS scheme?
It is led by the Government of Meghalaya, driven by the Office of the Chief Minister, and implemented on the ground through the **Department of Agriculture and Farmers' Empowerment** and the state's **Community & Rural Development (C&RD) Blocks**.

### 4. What is a Producer Group (PG)?
A Producer Group is a collective of farmers (or, in urban areas, other income-generating individuals) who come together to access the scheme's benefits as a group, even though the cash benefit is credited to individual members.

### 5. Who is eligible for FOCUS?
Farmers organised into (or willing to form) a Producer Group are the primary eligible unit. Rural farming households across Meghalaya were the original target group. From October 2022, the scheme was also extended to **urban Producer Groups** of 10 or more members engaged in a shared income-generating activity (e.g., weaving, pickle-making, or youth enterprise groups).

### 6. How much financial assistance does FOCUS provide?
Each Producer Group member is given an initial mobilisation fund of **₹5,000**, intended as seed/working capital to upscale their farming or income-generating activity.

### 7. Is the money a loan?
No. Government representatives have repeatedly clarified that the financial assistance under FOCUS is a **grant/assistance**, not a loan — it does not need to be repaid.

### 8. How is the money given out?
Funds have been disbursed both through **cheques distributed at public events** (especially in the scheme's earlier years) and through **direct transfer to beneficiaries' bank accounts**. The amount is credited to individual members of each registered Producer Group.

### 9. How do I register a Producer Group?
Farmers or eligible groups can register at their local **C&RD Block office**. Registration windows are announced periodically, and once a group is verified, funds are released in the subsequent disbursal round. The **MeghalayaOne portal** (meghalayaone.gov.in) is also used for registration and status tracking.

**9a. What documents are required to register?**
There's no single official document checklist published for FOCUS, but applicants and Producer Group members are generally expected to have:
- Proof of identity (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or another valid government photo ID)
- Meghalaya Family ID/Citizen ID (used to verify residency and household identity across various Meghalaya schemes)
- Proof of residence in the relevant village/block or locality
- Bank account details/passbook (for DBT or cheque payment)
- Producer Group formation/registration record showing members and shared activity
- A passport-size photograph, where required for local registration or the scheme card
- Any additional form specified by the local C&RD Block office for that registration round

Since exact requirements are set locally for each registration round, it's best to confirm the current list with your C&RD Block office or the MeghalayaOne portal before applying.

### 10. Can urban residents join FOCUS?
Yes. Since October 2022, urban Producer Groups of 10 or more members engaged in a shared income-generating activity — such as weaving, pickle-making, or other small-enterprise/youth-led activities — have been eligible to register and receive the same benefit as rural farmer groups.

### 11. What can the FOCUS funds be used for?
Members are encouraged to use the funds as seed/working capital to upscale their existing farming or income-generating activity. The Chief Minister has also encouraged groups to circulate part of the funds as a **"revolving fund"** among members so the benefit recirculates within the group rather than being a one-time payout.

### 12. What sectors does FOCUS focus on?
FOCUS supports several state-identified "Mission Mode" high-value agricultural sub-sectors, including:
- Lakadong turmeric
- Piggery
- Milk/dairy
- Spices
- Ginger
- Aroma (essential-oil) crops
- Poultry and horticulture

### 13. Does FOCUS only provide cash, or other support too?
Beyond the initial ₹5,000 mobilisation fund, FOCUS is described as providing broader support including value-chain development, machinery support, market linkages, transportation, and aggregation of produce — helping farm output move more efficiently "from farm to market."

### 14. How many farmers/households has FOCUS benefited?
Reported figures have grown over time: an early 2022 statement targeted over 4.5 lakh farmers with an estimated ₹200 crore budget; by mid-2025, cumulative disbursal was reported at around ₹120 crore to over 22,000 Producer Groups, benefiting roughly 2.1 lakh rural households across the state. These figures are updated periodically with each new disbursal round, so the latest official announcement should be checked for current numbers.

### 15. How many C&RD Blocks implement FOCUS?
FOCUS is rolled out through Meghalaya's Community & Rural Development (C&RD) Blocks. Reported block counts vary slightly by source — some cite 52, more recent (late-2025) reporting cites 56 — so this should be verified against the latest official figure.

### 16. Who currently heads the department responsible for FOCUS?
The scheme's agricultural components fall under the Department of Agriculture and Farmers' Empowerment / Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare, Meghalaya, whose minister as of recent reporting is Timothy D. Shira. The scheme itself is closely associated with Chief Minister Conrad K. Sangma, who has led most major FOCUS launch and disbursal events.

### 17. Where can I get official, up-to-date information about FOCUS?
- MeghalayaOne portal: meghalayaone.gov.in
- MyMeg portal: mymeg.meghalaya.gov.in
- Official Government of Meghalaya press releases (Office of the Chief Minister, Media & Communications Cell)
- The local C&RD Block office or the Department of Agriculture and Farmers' Empowerment

### 18. Why do some figures (launch date, budget, block count) vary between sources?
FOCUS does not have a single, centrally published statutory document (unlike a central Act-based scheme). Details are announced periodically through press releases, speeches, and disbursal events, so exact figures can vary slightly between reports and change over time as new tranches are announced. Always confirm time-sensitive figures against the most recent official government source.
