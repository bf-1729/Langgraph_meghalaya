# CM-ELEVATE (Meghalaya) — Complete Scheme Reference

## 1. Overview

**CM-ELEVATE** is the **Chief Minister's flagship credit-linked entrepreneurship programme** of the **Government of Meghalaya**, launched by Chief Minister **Conrad K. Sangma**. It is positioned as the **next step to the PRIME programme** that the state government launched roughly five years earlier, and is designed to create a conducive environment for entrepreneurs to accelerate **private-sector-led growth** across the state.

The programme stimulates economic development through increased credit flow, entrepreneurship development, and job creation, by offering **financial support (subsidy) of 35% to 75% of project cost** to selected applicants across a wide range of sectors — from agriculture and tourism to entertainment and wellness — combined with bank credit linkage, technology-provider linkage, and handholding/capacity-building support.

CM-ELEVATE functions as an **umbrella programme** made up of numerous individual sub-schemes (e.g., Piggery Scheme, Homestay Scheme, Agri Warehouse Scheme), each targeting a specific sector, but all administered under the same overall CM-ELEVATE brand, application portal, and subsidy framework.

### Legal Status: Scheme vs. Statutory Right
CM-ELEVATE is a **state government welfare/economic-development scheme implemented through executive decision and budgetary allocation**, not a law passed by the Meghalaya Legislative Assembly. There is **no individual legal entitlement** to the subsidy — receipt depends on application, eligibility screening, selection, and availability of funds under the relevant sub-scheme, rather than a court-enforceable guarantee.

## 2. Launch and Timeline

- **Launched on 13 October 2023** at an "Integrated" launch event in **Tura**, by Chief Minister Conrad K. Sangma, alongside distribution of cheques under the **YESS Meghalaya** initiative on the same day.
- Positioned as the **successor/next step to the PRIME programme**, which had run for approximately five years prior to CM-ELEVATE's launch.
- **11 March 2024:** Several sector-specific sub-schemes were notified/updated, including the Meghalaya Goat Farming Scheme, Meghalaya Agri Warehouse Scheme, Meghalaya CFC (Common Facility Centre) Scheme, Meghalaya Piggery Scheme, Meghalaya Poultry Scheme, and PRIME Agri-related schemes.
- **19 December 2025:** Further sub-schemes were notified/updated, including the Any Business Venture Scheme, Meghalaya Sports & Wellness Centre Scheme, and Meghalaya Cinema Theatre Scheme — indicating the programme continues to expand its sectoral coverage over time.
- The programme is designed to run over a multi-year horizon, with sources citing both a **3-year** period (in initial 2023 launch coverage) and a **5-year** period (in later PRIME Meghalaya programme framing) for its entrepreneur-reach target — reflecting either a revision of scope or differing framing across announcements; the current official figure should be confirmed from the latest source at the time of reading.

## 3. Objectives of the Programme

1. **Economic growth:** Accelerate and sustain economic development through entrepreneurship, job creation, infrastructure development, tourism promotion, and business investment.
2. **Increase credit flow:** Improve access to loans for aspiring individuals and businesses that face difficulty obtaining credit — especially marginalised communities and small and medium enterprises (SMEs).
3. **Encourage entrepreneurship:** Equip local youth with capital, knowledge, and skills through capacity-building programmes and training needed to successfully run their businesses.
4. **Increase employment:** Create jobs by encouraging entrepreneurship and helping existing businesses scale up capacity and operations.
5. **Encompass diverse needs:** Cater to varied sectors — agriculture, horticulture, animal husbandry, tourism, entertainment, wellbeing, power, and more.
6. **Single-window access:** Provide a unified online application platform (MeghalayaOne) for individuals and registered/unregistered entities to apply for support across any covered sector.
7. **Financial and handholding support:** Combine a direct subsidy (35–75% of project cost) with comprehensive handholding and capacity-building support, rather than a subsidy alone.
8. **Three-fold youth focus ("Engagement, Employment, Empowerment"):** As articulated at launch — engage youth actively in entrepreneurship, create substantial job opportunities for them, and empower them economically.

## 4. Programme Framing (Scale, as Publicly Stated)

- **Entrepreneur reach target:** More than **20,000 individuals/entrepreneurs**, cited across both the original 3-year framing and the later 5-year framing.
- **Investment scale:** Approximately **₹300 crore** over three years, as stated at the October 2023 launch.
- **Employment target:** Approximately **700 direct** employment opportunities, plus a range of indirect jobs, anticipated from the programme.
- **Applicant base:** Open to **individuals from Meghalaya**, **registered entities**, and **non-registered collectives** such as Self-Help Groups (SHGs), Village Organizations (VOs), and Producer Groups (PGs).
- **Subsidy range:** **35% to 75% of project cost**, varying by sub-scheme/sector, provided to reduce the credit risk entrepreneurs would otherwise bear when taking bank loans.

## 6. Eligibility Criteria

- **Residency:** Open to individuals/entities based in Meghalaya.
- **Applicant types:** Eligible applicants include:
  - Individuals
  - Registered business entities
  - Non-registered collectives, including **Self-Help Groups (SHGs)**, **Village Organizations (VOs)**, and **Producer Groups (PGs)**
- **Sector fit:** Applicants must be pursuing a business venture that falls under one of the 15 covered sectors (or the flexible "Any Business Venture" category, if their idea does not fit an existing named sub-scheme).
- **Credit-linkage requirement:** Because the programme is **credit-linked**, applicants are generally expected to access bank financing for their project, with the CM-ELEVATE subsidy (35–75% of project cost) reducing the credit risk/cost burden rather than fully funding the venture.
- Specific sub-schemes (e.g., Piggery, Poultry, Goat Farming, Polyhouse) may carry their own detailed eligibility conditions (minimum unit size, land/space requirements, prior experience, etc.) that are not fully detailed in the general programme-level sources reviewed — applicants should check the specific sub-scheme's notification on the MeghalayaOne portal or PRIME Meghalaya website for exact criteria.

## 7. Application and Registration Process

- **Single-window portal:** All applications for CM-ELEVATE are processed through **MeghalayaOne** (meghalayaone.gov.in), the Government of Meghalaya's unified e-services portal — described as a single online platform for individuals and registered/unregistered entities to apply for any business venture across the covered sectors.
- **PRIME Meghalaya as facilitating body:** The **PRIME (Promotion and Retention of Investment and Meghalaya's Entrepreneurs)** initiative — the state's entrepreneurship ecosystem body — is closely linked to CM-ELEVATE, listing beneficiary schemes, providing programme details, running training centres, and offering a PRIME Portal login for applicants/entrepreneurs.
- **Sub-scheme selection:** Applicants choose (or are guided toward) the specific sub-scheme matching their business sector at the time of application.
- **Bank linkage:** As a credit-linked programme, the process involves linking the applicant with a partner bank for the loan component of the project, alongside the government subsidy component.
- **Handholding and capacity building:** Beyond financial support, PRIME/CM-ELEVATE provides capacity-building programmes, training, and technology-provider linkages to help entrepreneurs execute their projects.

### 7.1 Required Documents
No single consolidated official document checklist covering all 15 CM-ELEVATE sub-schemes was found in the sources reviewed; each sub-scheme may specify its own list on the MeghalayaOne portal at the time of application. Commonly expected documents for a credit-linked entrepreneurship subsidy programme of this kind typically include:
- Proof of identity (e.g., Aadhaar card)
- Proof of Meghalaya residency
- Bank account details
- Business/project proposal or plan
- Land/premises documents (for schemes requiring physical infrastructure, e.g., Polyhouse, Piggery, CFC, Agri Warehouse)
- Registration certificate (for registered entities, SHGs, VOs, or PGs applying as a collective)
- Quotations/cost estimates for the proposed project (to determine the subsidy amount as a percentage of project cost)

## 8. Financial Assistance Structure

- **Subsidy range:** **35% to 75% of total project cost**, varying by sector/sub-scheme.
- **Nature of support:** A capital subsidy that reduces the entrepreneur's own funding burden and the risk associated with taking on a bank loan for the remaining project cost, rather than a fully-funded grant.
- **Sector-specific example:** Under the Agriculture Department's polyhouse component, **50% financial aid** is specifically cited for polyhouse construction to support high-value vegetable and floriculture cultivation.
- **Complementary support:** Alongside the subsidy, the programme provides bank credit linkage, technology-provider linkage, and capacity-building/training support — positioned as equally important to the cash subsidy in enabling entrepreneurs to execute their projects successfully.

## 9. Implementing Institutions

- **PRIME (Promotion and Retention of Investment and Meghalaya's Entrepreneurs):** The state's entrepreneurship promotion body, which runs the CM-ELEVATE public-facing page, beneficiary listings, training centres, and portal login — describing CM-ELEVATE as the "next step" to the original PRIME programme.
- **Meghalaya Basin Development Authority (MBDA):** Involved in related livelihood/rural-collective ecosystem programmes (e.g., FOCUS+, PROGRESS) whose participant base (SHGs, VOs, PGs) overlaps with CM-ELEVATE's eligible applicant categories.
- **Relevant line departments:** Individual sub-schemes are administered in coordination with the relevant state department for that sector — e.g., the **Tourism Department** for the Green Villa, Homestay, Motorhome, and Tourism Vehicle schemes; the **Agriculture Department** for the Polyhouse scheme; and other departments for sectors like Piggery, Poultry, Goat Farming, Dairy, Sericulture & Weaving, Cinema Theatre, and Sports & Wellness.
- **Chief Minister's Office:** CM-ELEVATE is publicly positioned as a personal flagship initiative of Chief Minister Conrad K. Sangma, who has led major launch events himself.
- **Partner banks:** Public and private sector banks are engaged as credit-linkage partners to provide the loan component alongside the government subsidy.

## 10. Convergence with Other Meghalaya Government Schemes/Programmes

- **PRIME (predecessor programme):** CM-ELEVATE is explicitly framed as building on and extending the earlier PRIME programme's entrepreneurship push.
- **YESS Meghalaya:** Cheque distribution under the YESS Meghalaya youth initiative took place at the same event as the CM-ELEVATE launch in Tura, reflecting a shared youth-entrepreneurship push across schemes.
- **FOCUS+ / PROGRESS / Community Investment Fund:** These rural livelihood schemes share overlapping applicant categories (SHGs, VOs, Producer Groups) with CM-ELEVATE, though they operate as separate schemes with their own benefit structures (see the FOCUS+ reference document for details).
- **Green Meghalaya initiative:** Cited alongside CM-ELEVATE coverage as a related sustainability-linked programme that has benefited individuals in Garo Hills.

## 12. Complete End-to-End Process Flow

Based on how the programme has been described across launch coverage and the official PRIME Meghalaya page, the lifecycle of a CM-ELEVATE application broadly follows these stages:

1. **Sector/sub-scheme identification:** The applicant identifies which of the 15 covered sectors (or the flexible "Any Business Venture" category) matches their proposed business.
2. **Application via MeghalayaOne:** The applicant applies through the single-window MeghalayaOne portal, selecting the relevant CM-ELEVATE sub-scheme.
3. **Documentation submission:** Required documents (identity, residency, project proposal, land/premises papers where applicable, registration proof for collectives) are submitted along with the application.
4. **Screening/eligibility check:** The relevant department or PRIME Meghalaya screens the application against the sub-scheme's specific eligibility criteria.
5. **Bank credit linkage:** As a credit-linked programme, the applicant is connected with a partner bank to arrange financing for the portion of project cost not covered by subsidy.
6. **Subsidy sanction:** Upon approval, the government subsidy (35–75% of project cost, or the sub-scheme-specific rate, e.g., 50% for Polyhouse) is sanctioned.
7. **Capacity building/handholding:** The entrepreneur receives training, technology-provider linkages, and other handholding support from PRIME Meghalaya to help set up and run the venture.
8. **Project implementation:** The entrepreneur executes the project (e.g., constructing a polyhouse, setting up a piggery unit, acquiring a tourism vehicle) using the combined subsidy and loan funding.
9. **Disbursement/verification events:** Larger disbursements and scheme milestones are periodically marked at public events, sometimes attended by the Chief Minister or other senior officials (e.g., a Union Finance Minister visit to a CM-ELEVATE event has been documented).
10. **Ongoing monitoring:** PRIME Meghalaya's stated functions include "Monitoring & Evaluation" of supported ventures, alongside market linkage support for the resulting businesses.

**Note:** This flow is reconstructed from the official PRIME Meghalaya CM-ELEVATE page and press coverage of the programme's launch, not from a single published end-to-end process manual, and specific steps/timing may vary by sub-scheme.

## 13. Glossary of Scheme-Related Terms

- **CM-ELEVATE:** The Chief Minister's flagship credit-linked entrepreneurship programme of Meghalaya, launched October 2023, offering 35–75% subsidy on project cost across 15 sectors.
- **PRIME (Promotion and Retention of Investment and Meghalaya's Entrepreneurs):** Meghalaya's entrepreneurship ecosystem body, whose earlier standalone programme CM-ELEVATE builds upon; also the organisation running training centres, incubation, and the PRIME Portal.
- **PRIME Rural:** A PRIME Meghalaya vertical focused on rural entrepreneurship (referenced in PRIME's site navigation alongside CM-ELEVATE).
- **MeghalayaOne portal:** The state government's unified e-services portal (meghalayaone.gov.in) used as the single-window application platform for CM-ELEVATE and other Meghalaya schemes.
- **Self-Help Group (SHG):** A village-level collective of individuals (often women) pooling resources for savings/credit and income-generating activity; one of the eligible non-registered-entity applicant categories under CM-ELEVATE.
- **Village Organization (VO):** A federation of multiple SHGs at the village level; another eligible applicant category under CM-ELEVATE.
- **Producer Group (PG):** A collective of members undertaking a shared income-generating activity; an eligible applicant category under CM-ELEVATE, and the same unit type used in the FOCUS/FOCUS+ schemes.
- **Common Facility Centre (CFC):** A shared production/processing facility that allows multiple micro-enterprises to use common infrastructure without individual capital investment; also the name of one CM-ELEVATE sub-scheme.
- **Credit-linked programme:** A scheme design where government subsidy is paired with (and depends on) the applicant also securing bank financing, rather than providing a stand-alone grant.
- **CM – Regional Transport Scheme:** A CM-ELEVATE-linked scheme providing financial aid to entrepreneurs operating bus services between Block HQs and District HQs.
