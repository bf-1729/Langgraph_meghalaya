# FOCUS+ (Focus Plus) — General FAQ

### What is FOCUS+?
FOCUS+ (also written "Focus Plus") is a rural-livelihoods and farmer cash-benefit scheme of the **Government of Meghalaya**. It provides an individual cash benefit of **₹5,000 per household**, paid by Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the beneficiary's own bank account, to households that are registered members of a Producer Group. It is driven by the Chief Minister's Office and implemented mainly through the **Meghalaya Basin Development Authority (MBDA)**.

### Is FOCUS+ a central scheme like MGNREGA or PMAY?
No. FOCUS+ is a **state government welfare scheme** run through executive decision and annual budgetary allocation — not a law of the Meghalaya Legislative Assembly and not a Government of India programme. There is **no individual legal entitlement**: receipt depends on being a registered Producer Group member, block-level verification, and funds being available in a given disbursement cycle.

### When was FOCUS+ launched?
It was launched on **28 July 2022** at an event in **Resubelpara, North Garo Hills**, by Chief Minister Conrad K. Sangma, with the stated aim of reaching every farming household in the state.

### What is the objective of FOCUS+?
To give direct household-level financial support for farm inputs and additional income-generating activities, encourage livelihood diversification (piggery, poultry, horticulture, spices such as Lakadong turmeric, ginger, milk, aroma/essential oils), and route all payments through DBT for transparency and financial inclusion.

### Who is eligible for FOCUS+?
A household must be a resident of Meghalaya in a village or block covered by the rollout, and must be (or become) a registered member of a **Producer Group** of 10 or more members undertaking a shared income-generating activity. Registration windows open periodically, so eligibility for a given cycle's payment depends on registering within that cycle. There is no published income-threshold or asset test beyond Producer Group membership.

### What is a Producer Group?
A collective of **10 or more members** (farmers or households) engaged in a shared income-generating activity — farming, livestock rearing, horticulture, weaving, food processing, and similar. Producer Group membership is a precondition for the FOCUS+ individual benefit.

### How does someone apply for FOCUS+?
The household first joins a registered Producer Group, then registers individually for the FOCUS+ benefit — on the ground at Community & Rural Development (C&RD) block-level camps and, in recent cycles, online on the **MeghalayaOne portal** (meghalayaone.gov.in). Details are verified at the block level before funds are sanctioned.

### What documents are typically required?
Proof of identity (e.g. Aadhaar), proof of Meghalaya residency, bank account details/passbook for the DBT credit, Producer Group registration/membership proof, and a passport-size photograph.

### How much money does FOCUS+ pay, and how?
₹5,000 per household, credited by Direct Benefit Transfer into the beneficiary's own bank account. In some cycles it has been released in tranches/installments rather than one lump sum — for example a first installment of ₹2,500 for FY 2023–24, with the balance to follow. Beneficiaries receive an SMS confirming the credit.

### What is the FOCUS+ card?
An identification card issued to a verified beneficiary household as proof of registration/beneficiary status, usually handed out at district- or block-level government events.

### Who implements FOCUS+?
The **Meghalaya Basin Development Authority (MBDA)** is the principal implementing agency, working through C&RD Blocks. The scheme is publicly positioned as a flagship initiative of the Chief Minister's Office. Parts of the broader ecosystem it sits within draw on a convergence fund involving the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA), the Chief Minister's Special Development Fund, and MBDA's own resources.

### What other Meghalaya schemes is FOCUS+ disbursed alongside?
It is often promoted and paid out at the same events as **PROGRESS** (livestock — piglets, Kuroiler chicks), **Yess Meghalaya** (youth group grants up to ₹1 lakh), the **Community Investment Fund (CIF)** for Self-Help Groups, **MegLIFE** (community infrastructure), and mission-mode agri-projects in turmeric, piggery, milk, spices, ginger and aroma oils.

### What reported figures exist for FOCUS+?
Point-in-time figures from Chief Minister's Office press releases and event coverage: ₹26 crore to 53,981 producers on Meghalaya Farmers' Day (December 2022); a stated 2022–23 target of reaching 4.5 lakh farmers; and, as of August 2025, cumulative disbursement of about ₹75.3 crore to over 1.5 lakh farmers, including a FY 2023–24 first installment of ₹23.25 crore (₹2,500 each) to more than 93,000 beneficiaries. Because the scheme runs in recurring annual cycles, cumulative totals are point-in-time as of the cited event, not a final figure.

### How is FOCUS+ different from PMAY-G and MGNREGA in this assistant's data?
FOCUS+ is a **cash-disbursement** scheme: one record is one payment to a member in a tranche (not one house and not one household with work). It is the only person-level dataset held, it has **no date field** finer than the financial year, and its **money unit is unverified** in the warehouse — figures are reported with that caveat. Amounts, installment structure and registration windows are re-announced each cycle, so any time-sensitive figure should be checked against the latest official press release or MBDA notification.

### Where can official, up-to-date FOCUS+ information be checked?
The Chief Minister's Office, Government of Meghalaya press releases (meghalaya.gov.in); the Meghalaya Basin Development Authority website (mbda.gov.in); and the MeghalayaOne portal (meghalayaone.gov.in) for registration.
