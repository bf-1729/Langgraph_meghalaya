# FOCUS+ (Meghalaya) — Complete Scheme Reference

## 1. Overview

**FOCUS+** is a flagship rural-livelihoods and farmer cash-benefit scheme of the **Government of Meghalaya**, driven by Chief Minister **Conrad K. Sangma** and implemented mainly through the **Meghalaya Basin Development Authority (MBDA)**. It provides an **individual cash benefit of ₹5,000 per household**, transferred directly to the beneficiary's bank account, to households that are registered members of a Producer Group. Beneficiaries are also issued a **FOCUS+ card** as identification.

FOCUS+ was introduced to extend benefits to **all farming households** across the state and to let families take up additional income-generating activities beyond their core farming activity — not just households already receiving group-level support.

### Legal Status: Scheme vs. Statutory Right
FOCUS+ is a **state government welfare scheme implemented through executive decision and annual budgetary allocation**, not a law passed by the Meghalaya Legislative Assembly. There is **no individual legal entitlement** to the benefit — receipt depends on being identified/registered as a Producer Group member, verified by the implementing authority, and on funds being available in a given disbursement cycle, rather than on a court-enforceable guarantee.

## 2. Launch and Timeline

- **Launched on 28 July 2022** at an event in **Resubelpara, North Garo Hills**, by Chief Minister Conrad K. Sangma, intended to reach **all farming households** across the state and let families take up additional income-generating activities.
- **December 2022 (Meghalaya Farmers' Day):** A single-day disbursement of **₹26 crore to 53,981 producers** was made under FOCUS+ by the click of a button, as part of a stated target of reaching **4.5 lakh farmers** that year.
- **August 2025:** CM Sangma released the **first installment (₹2,500) for FY 2023–24** to more than 93,000 beneficiaries under FOCUS+ (₹23.25 crore in that tranche alone), noting that FOCUS+ had by then supported over **1.5 lakh farmers** with a cumulative **₹75.3 crore** disbursed, and urged farmers to register on the **MeghalayaOne portal**.
- The scheme continues to run in annual cycles, with fresh registration windows and installments announced periodically at district-level events.

## 3. Objectives of the Scheme

1. **Direct household-level financial support:** Extend a direct, individual cash benefit to every registered farming household, to help meet immediate input costs (seeds, livestock, tools) and household needs.
2. **Diversification of livelihoods:** Encourage households to take up additional income-generating activities beyond their primary farming activity, supporting sectors such as piggery, poultry, horticulture, spices (including Lakadong turmeric), milk, ginger, and aroma/essential oils.
3. **Financial inclusion and transparency:** Route all payments through **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)** into the beneficiary's own bank account, with an identity card (FOCUS+ card) and digital registration (MeghalayaOne portal) to reduce leakage and improve targeting.
4. **Universal household coverage:** Extend benefits beyond just Producer Group-level support to reach every eligible member household individually.
5. **State development vision:** Contribute to Meghalaya's stated goal of being among **India's top 10 states within a decade**, given that farmers make up a large share (officials have cited around 70%) of the state's population.

## 4. Nature of the Benefit

- **Unit of benefit:** Individual household (must be a registered Producer Group member).
- **Benefit amount:** ₹5,000 per household, credited to the beneficiary's individual bank account.
- **Purpose:** Individual/household-level support; funds for farm inputs and additional income-generating activities.
- **Identification:** A **FOCUS+ card** is issued to the household as proof of registration/beneficiary status.
- **Sectors covered:** Broad agricultural value chain — piggery, poultry, horticulture, spices, ginger, milk, aroma/essential oils, and other farm/allied and non-farm income-generating activities.

Registration as a member of a Producer Group is a **precondition** for a household to access the FOCUS+ benefit.

## 5. Eligibility Criteria

- **Residency:** Applicant/household must be a resident of Meghalaya, registered in a village/block covered under the scheme's rollout.
- **Producer Group membership:** To receive the FOCUS+ individual cash benefit, a household must be (or become) a registered member of a **Producer Group** comprising **10 or more members** engaged in a shared income-generating activity — farming, livestock rearing (e.g., piggery, poultry), horticulture, or non-farm activities such as weaving or food processing (e.g., a "pickle-making group"). Youth groups undertaking income-generating activities are also eligible to form Producer Groups.
- **Urban and rural coverage:** The benefit, originally rural-focused, was extended to urban households engaged in similar collective income-generating activities as well.
- **One-time/cycle-based registration:** Registration windows are opened periodically (e.g., a registration deadline of 30 November was announced in one cycle, with fund transfers following in the first week of December), so eligibility for a given year's disbursement depends on registering within that cycle's window.
- Beyond the core "PG membership" condition, publicly available official material does not lay out a detailed income-threshold or asset-based eligibility test (unlike, for example, PMAY's EWS/LIG/MIG income categories) — the scheme appears to be broadly targeted at Meghalaya's farming and rural livelihood population rather than restricted by a formal means test.

## 6. Application and Registration Process

- **Producer Group membership (precondition):** A household must belong to a registered Producer Group of 10 or more members before it can register for FOCUS+.
- **Individual registration for FOCUS+:** Once part of a registered PG, individual households register for the FOCUS+ cash benefit. In recent cycles, the Government has directed farmers to register on the **MeghalayaOne portal** (meghalayaone.gov.in), the state's citizen-facing digital services platform, alongside on-ground registration facilitated at Community & Rural Development (C&RD) Block-level events.
- **Verification:** Household registration details are verified at the block level before funds are sanctioned and disbursed.
- **Card issuance:** Verified beneficiary households are issued a **FOCUS+ card**, distributed at district/block-level government events, serving as proof of registration/beneficiary status.
- **Bank account linkage:** Beneficiaries must have a bank account for the cash transfer to be credited; disbursements are announced and pushed out in bulk (e.g., via SMS confirmation to beneficiaries' phones at public events where the Chief Minister triggers the transfer).

### 6.1 Required Documents
- Proof of identity (e.g., Aadhaar card)
- Proof of Meghalaya residency
- Bank account details/passbook (for DBT credit)
- Producer Group registration/membership proof
- Passport-size photograph (commonly required for beneficiary cards in comparable state schemes)

## 7. Financial Assistance and Disbursement Mechanism

- **Amount:** ₹5,000 per household under FOCUS+.
- **Mode of payment:** **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)** straight into the beneficiary's bank account — no cash handling by intermediaries.
- **Installment-based release:** Rather than always being paid as a single lump sum, FOCUS+ funds have in some cycles been released in **tranches/installments** — for example, a first installment of ₹2,500 (half the total ₹5,000) was released for FY 2023–24, with the remaining balance to follow.
- **Public disbursement events:** Large-scale disbursement is often carried out symbolically at public events, where the Chief Minister triggers a bulk transfer (e.g., ₹26 crore to 53,981 producers on Meghalaya Farmers' Day 2022, or ₹23.25 crore to 93,000+ beneficiaries in August 2025), with beneficiaries receiving an SMS confirmation on their registered mobile number.
- **Scale of investment:** The Government has referenced a large annual investment behind its combined farmer-welfare initiatives (including FOCUS+), with a projection to channel significant funds to Meghalaya's farmers over the following decade.

## 8. Implementing Institutions

- **Meghalaya Basin Development Authority (MBDA):** The principal state agency implementing FOCUS+, alongside its other livelihood and agri-business programmes (e.g., MLAMP, PRIME, Green Meghalaya).
- **Community & Rural Development (C&RD) Blocks:** The scheme is rolled out and monitored block-wise; district/block-level events are used for registration, card distribution, and disbursement.
- **Chief Minister's Office:** FOCUS+ is publicly positioned as a personal flagship initiative of Chief Minister Conrad K. Sangma, who has led most major disbursement and launch events himself.
- **Convergence funding partners:** Some components of the broader ecosystem FOCUS+ sits within draw on a **convergence fund** involving the **Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA)**, the **Chief Minister's Special Development Fund**, and MBDA's own resources.

## 9. Convergence with Other Meghalaya Government Schemes

FOCUS+ is typically promoted and disbursed alongside several other Meghalaya state schemes at the same public events, reflecting a convergence approach to rural welfare:

- **PROGRESS scheme:** Provides livestock (e.g., piglets and Kuroiler chicks) to beneficiaries to encourage sustainable, income-generating livestock farming — piggery in particular is highlighted as a high-potential sector given the state's large pork consumption, much of which is currently imported from other states.
- **Yess Meghalaya:** A youth-focused initiative providing up to **₹1 lakh per group** to youth organisations for various income-generating activities.
- **Community Investment Fund (CIF):** Channels funds to village-level Self-Help Groups (SHGs) for entrepreneurial activities (e.g., ₹2.15 crore disbursed to SHGs in one district in a single cycle).
- **MegLIFE:** A financial support scheme that has, for example, funded construction of Community Halls in villages through a convergence of JICA, the Chief Minister's Special Development Fund, and MBDA.
- **Mission-mode agri-projects:** Sector-specific initiatives in Lakadong turmeric, piggery, milk, spices, ginger, and aroma/essential oils, which FOCUS+ funding is meant to support and upscale.

## 11. Complete End-to-End Process Flow

Based on how the scheme has been described across launch and disbursement events, the lifecycle of a FOCUS+ benefit broadly follows these stages:

1. **Producer Group membership:** The household must belong to (or join) a Producer Group of 10 or more members undertaking a shared income-generating activity (farming, livestock, horticulture, weaving, food processing, etc.), often formed with facilitation at the C&RD Block level.
2. **Individual registration for FOCUS+:** The household registers individually for the FOCUS+ benefit — through on-ground registration at block-level camps/events and, in recent cycles, via the **MeghalayaOne portal**.
3. **Verification:** Block-level officials verify each member household's registration details before sanction.
4. **Sanction and card issuance:** Verified households are issued a **FOCUS+ card** as proof of beneficiary status, typically distributed at a district/block public event.
5. **Bank account linkage:** Beneficiary bank account details are captured/confirmed to enable Direct Benefit Transfer.
6. **Fund release (DBT):** The ₹5,000 household benefit is credited via DBT — sometimes as a single transfer, sometimes in installments (e.g., an initial ₹2,500 tranche followed by the balance).
7. **Public disbursement/confirmation:** Large batches of transfers are often triggered together at a public event led by the Chief Minister, with beneficiaries receiving an SMS confirming the credit to their registered mobile number.
8. **Utilisation:** Households use the funds for the stated purpose — farm inputs, livestock, equipment, or other income-generating activities.
9. **Next cycle:** Registration reopens in subsequent years/cycles, with new households able to join, and existing beneficiaries potentially receiving further installments or renewed support.

**Note:** This flow is reconstructed from press coverage of the scheme's operation, not from a published official process manual (see Section 13), and specific step order/timing may vary by district or cycle.

## 12. Glossary of Scheme-Related Terms

- **FOCUS+:** Meghalaya's individual household cash-benefit scheme, launched July 2022, providing ₹5,000 per household to registered Producer Group members.
- **Producer Group (PG):** A collective of 10 or more members (farmers or households) undertaking a shared income-generating activity; PG membership is a precondition for the FOCUS+ benefit.
- **FOCUS+ card:** An identification card issued to verified beneficiary households under FOCUS+, serving as proof of registration/beneficiary status.
- **DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer):** The mechanism by which scheme funds are credited electronically and directly into a beneficiary's own bank account, without intermediaries.
- **MBDA (Meghalaya Basin Development Authority):** The state government agency that implements FOCUS+ along with several other Meghalaya livelihood and agri-business programmes.
- **C&RD Block (Community & Rural Development Block):** The block-level administrative unit through which the scheme is rolled out, monitored, and used as the venue for registration, verification, and disbursement events.
- **MeghalayaOne portal:** The state government's citizen-facing digital services platform (meghalayaone.gov.in) used for scheme registration, including FOCUS+.
- **PROGRESS scheme:** A related Meghalaya livestock-livelihood scheme (piglets, Kuroiler chicks) often disbursed alongside FOCUS+ at the same events.
- **Yess Meghalaya:** A Meghalaya youth-focused livelihood scheme providing group grants (up to ₹1 lakh) for youth income-generating activities.
- **Community Investment Fund (CIF):** Funding channeled to village-level Self-Help Groups (SHGs) for entrepreneurial activities, distinct from but often disbursed alongside FOCUS+.
- **MegLIFE:** A Meghalaya scheme providing financial support for community infrastructure (e.g., village Community Halls), funded through a convergence of JICA, the Chief Minister's Special Development Fund, and MBDA.

## 13. Where to Find Authoritative, Current Information

- The **Chief Minister's Office, Government of Meghalaya** press releases (meghalaya.gov.in) are the most authoritative source for launch details, disbursement figures, and scheme announcements.
- The **Meghalaya Basin Development Authority (MBDA)** official website (mbda.gov.in) lists MBDA's programmes and schemes, procurement notices, and related livelihood initiatives that FOCUS+ sits alongside.
- The **MeghalayaOne portal** (meghalayaone.gov.in) is the citizen-facing platform farmers have been directed to for registration.
- Because **benefit amounts, installment structure, registration windows, and cumulative achievement figures are revised and re-announced each cycle**, any time-sensitive figure in this document should be verified against the latest official press release or MBDA notification rather than treated as permanently fixed.
