# PMAY-Gramin — General FAQ

### What is PMAY-Gramin?
PMAY-Gramin (PMAY-G) is the rural component of the Government of India's flagship affordable housing scheme, launched to achieve "Housing for All" by providing financial assistance to help eligible rural households build or improve a permanent (pucca) home.

### When was PMAY-Gramin launched?
PMAY-Gramin (PMAY-G) was launched in 2016, replacing the earlier Indira Awaas Yojana with a more transparent, data-driven framework, implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development.

### What is the main objective of PMAY-Gramin?
The core objective is "Housing for All" — ensuring every eligible rural family has access to a permanent, pucca house with basic amenities, thereby improving living standards and promoting inclusive rural development.

### What are the key benefits of PMAY-Gramin?
- Financial assistance for constructing a new house.
- Basic amenities support, such as access to toilets, electricity, LPG, and drinking water connections, often converged with other government schemes.
- Encouragement of home ownership in the name of women, or joint ownership between spouses.
- Improved living standards and social/economic security for low-income and vulnerable rural families.
- Priority for the homeless, manual scavengers, SC/ST households, minorities, persons with disabilities, and other vulnerable groups.

### Who is eligible for PMAY-Gramin?
In general, an applicant is eligible if their household does not already own a pucca (permanent) house anywhere in India, and they have not previously availed central government housing assistance. PMAY-G specifically targets rural households identified as houseless or living in kutcha (temporary/dilapidated) houses, based on deprivation criteria from the Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) and subsequent verified data (the Awaas+ list), with priority given to SC/ST households, manual scavengers, minorities, persons with disabilities, and other vulnerable groups.

### What is a "pucca house" and why does it matter for eligibility?
A pucca house is a permanent, all-weather dwelling built with durable materials (as opposed to a kutcha house made of temporary materials like mud or thatch). Not owning a pucca house anywhere in India is a core eligibility condition for PMAY-G, since the scheme targets households currently without adequate permanent housing.

### Can a family that has already received government housing assistance apply again?
No. A core eligibility condition is that the family must not have previously availed central assistance under any housing scheme from the Government of India or a state government, and must not have already received a PMAY subsidy.

### How does someone apply for PMAY-Gramin?
PMAY-G applications are typically processed through Gram Panchayats using the Awaas+ beneficiary list; eligible households are identified from the SECC/Awaas+ data rather than through an individual online application in the usual sense.

### What documents are typically required for PMAY-Gramin?
Commonly required details include Aadhaar details, proof of not owning a pucca house, and bank account details. PMAY-G applicants may also need to link their MGNREGA job card and Swachh Bharat Mission registration, since house construction assistance under PMAY-G is often converged with these schemes.

### How is the house construction assistance funded?
PMAY-Gramin is funded jointly by the central and state governments, with the exact cost-sharing ratio differing between general-category and hilly/northeastern/special-category states.

### Who implements PMAY-Gramin?
PMAY-Gramin is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development in partnership with state governments and Gram Panchayats.

### Does PMAY-Gramin promote women's ownership of housing?
Yes. The scheme encourages allotment of the house in the name of the female head of the household, or joint ownership between husband and wife, as a way to strengthen women's property rights and financial inclusion.

### What basic amenities are linked with PMAY-Gramin houses?
PMAY-Gramin construction is often converged with other government schemes to ensure beneficiary houses have basic amenities such as a toilet (Swachh Bharat Mission), electricity connection (Saubhagya), LPG connection (Ujjwala Yojana), and drinking water access.

### How can an applicant check their PMAY-Gramin application or beneficiary status?
Applicants can check their status online on the official PMAY-Gramin portal using their beneficiary ID, Aadhaar number, or registered mobile number, or by contacting their local Gram Panchayat.

### Where can official, up-to-date PMAY-Gramin information be checked?
The official PMAY-Gramin portal (pmayg.nic.in), maintained by the Ministry of Rural Development, is the authoritative source for current eligibility criteria, unit assistance amounts, and application status.
